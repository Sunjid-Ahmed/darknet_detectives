// 02 — ABOUT THE COLLECTIVE

    
We don't play the game.
We reverse-engineer it.

    
Darknet Detectives is a three-member CTF collective operating at the intersection of curiosity and chaos. Every challenge is a puzzle waiting to be unpacked — every flag, a story we're destined to tell.

  


  

    

      

      
OFFENSIVE_SECURITY

      
From initial recon to privilege escalation, we map attack surfaces with surgical precision. Web, network, or host — every vector is fair game.

    

    

      

      
CRYPTANALYSIS

      
RSA, ECC, lattice-based — we break what others consider unbreakable. From classical ciphers to modern schemes, no encryption survives our scrutiny.

    

    

      

      
FORENSICS & OSINT

      
Memory dumps, packet captures, steganographic payloads — we read the digital aftermath like a native language. Every byte tells a story.
